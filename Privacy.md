# Privacy Policy — Vitals

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Vitals is a private personal dashboard operated by Gary Yelton for his own use.

## Who this is for
This app is not a public product. I am the only user. If you reached this page from a WHOOP authorization screen, you are authorizing Vitals to read WHOOP data so it can be shown on my local dashboard.

## Data we receive from WHOOP
If you click Allow, Vitals may read:
- Recovery score, HRV, and resting heart rate
- Physiological cycles and day strain
- Sleep duration and sleep performance
- Basic profile information needed to complete OAuth

Vitals does not request workout GPS or journal text.

## How the data is used
- To display charts and daily summaries for the account owner
- To store history in a local database on the machine running Vitals

The data is not sold, not advertised against, and not shared with other companies.

## Where it is stored
Tokens and WHOOP metrics are stored on the computer that runs Vitals (local SQLite). They are not uploaded to a separate analytics vendor.

## What we do not do
- No accounts for other people
- No marketing emails
- No selling or renting data
- No training public AI models on your WHOOP data

## Retention
Data stays until the account owner deletes the local database or revokes access.

## How to revoke access
You can revoke Vitals at any time in the WHOOP app under connected apps / integrations. After revoke, Vitals should no longer pull new WHOOP data. You can also ask the operator to delete stored tokens and cached metrics.
